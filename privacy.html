Nth Reader Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026 · Developer: Brad Redenbach
Android application: com.nthreader.app

Nth Reader lets you read your own books and comics, organize a virtual bookshelf, personalize its appearance, and listen using your device's text-to-speech service. This policy explains the Android app's data handling, along with optional services you choose to use.

Data stored on your device

The app processes files you select, including books, comics, documents, cover images, and photos used in decorative frames. It stores imported content, library metadata, reading positions, reading and voice preferences, shelf arrangements, and decorations in local app storage. These are used to provide reading, customization, and backup features. Narration text and progress may be stored locally to resume reading aloud. If you enable the bookshelf widget, shelf images and layout data are stored locally and displayed through your Android launcher.

Nth Reader does not require an app account. The Android app does not include advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated tracking service, and does not upload your library to a developer-operated server. Brad Redenbach does not sell your personal data.

Read-aloud voices

Starting with version 1.0.2, read-aloud selects only installed voices that your Android text-to-speech engine reports as not requiring a network connection. The web edition likewise selects only voices the browser identifies as local. The app passes book text to that local speech service for narration. It does not select network voices or fall back to an unspecified default voice. If no eligible offline voice is available, narration stops and asks you to install one through your device settings.

Your speech engine is separate software provided by your device or another provider. Nth Reader relies on its voice capability reporting and does not control that software's independent network activity. Downloading voice data through device settings may require internet access and is governed by the provider's terms. Versions before 1.0.2 could use network voices, which may transmit text and technical information to the speech provider; update the app for offline-only voice selection.

Files, backups, and external services

You choose files through Android's file picker. If you select a cloud-storage provider, that provider handles access under its own terms. Exports and backups are written to the destination you choose; they can include your books, images, progress, and settings. Treat exported backups as private files. They are not protected by an app-specific backup password.

The app allows Android system backup. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your device provider may back up or transfer app data. Manage those copies through your device or backup provider's settings. Nth Reader does not operate its own cloud-sync service.

The Android reading interface and its dependencies are bundled in the app. Links you open in another app or browser are handled by that app or website. The web edition and this public policy page are hosted by GitHub Pages; when you visit them, GitHub receives connection information such as your IP address and browser request details under GitHub's privacy statement. Google Play may process installation, purchase, diagnostic, and testing information under Google's privacy policy. The developer may see reports Google Play makes available, such as crash reports, aggregate statistics, and feedback you submit.

Permissions and visibility

File access is used for content you select. Notification and foreground media-playback permissions support read-aloud controls and background narration; a wake lock supports continued playback. Internet access is declared for supported network functionality. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions. Narration notifications and the optional bookshelf widget can show book information on your device, including on surfaces visible to other people.

Retention and deletion

Local library data remains until you remove it or clear the app's storage. Use the app's removal controls for individual library items. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Nth Reader → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. These actions do not remove original files outside app storage, exported backups, or copies held by a system or cloud backup provider; delete those separately. There is no Nth Reader account to delete.

If you contact the developer, your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send are used to respond and troubleshoot. Avoid sending private book content or unnecessary personal information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of support messages using the contact below, subject to those obligations.

Security and your choices

Local data relies on Android's app storage protections and your device security. The app does not add separate encryption to exported backups. Protect your device, select trusted speech and file providers, and choose secure backup destinations. No storage or transfer method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact Brad Redenbach at annthexperience@gmail.com. This policy will be updated when relevant practices change; the effective date above identifies the current version.
